# Product Features

This document outlines the major product areas in FlowDesk. The sections describe intended capabilities and development scope for the platform's public roadmap.

## 1. Unified Inbox

### Purpose
Provide one operational inbox for customer communication across supported channels.

### Intended Functionality
- Consolidate conversations from channels such as SMS and email.
- Maintain conversation timelines with relevant customer context.
- Support templates, attachments, and communication history references.
- Preserve account-scoped visibility and permissions.

### Practical Use Cases
- A front desk team responds to inbound appointment questions without switching tools.
- Staff reuses approved templates for common service reminders.
- A manager reviews call and message context before customer follow-up.

### Relevant Integrations
- Twilio for communications channel connectivity.

## 2. Smart Booking

### Purpose
Coordinate appointment operations with clearer scheduling rules and staff alignment.

### Intended Functionality
- Appointment scheduling with configurable availability windows.
- Staff assignment support based on capacity or role constraints.
- Service-area aware booking policies.
- Reminder workflows tied to appointment events.

### Practical Use Cases
- A customer books a service slot that respects location and team availability.
- A coordinator reassigns appointments to balance team workload.
- Reminder workflows reduce no-shows and late confirmations.

### Relevant Integrations
- Calendar integrations for schedule synchronization (scope and providers evolving).

## 3. Customer CRM

### Purpose
Maintain reliable customer records for communication and operational continuity.

### Intended Functionality
- Structured profiles with contact details and addresses.
- Notes and tags for segmentation and internal coordination.
- Interaction history connected to communications and bookings.
- Account-scoped customer data management.

### Practical Use Cases
- Staff quickly sees prior interactions before responding to a customer.
- Teams segment customers for targeted follow-up.
- Operational notes reduce repeated intake questions.

## 4. Workflow Automation

### Purpose
Reduce repetitive manual tasks by automating high-frequency communication workflows.

### Intended Functionality
- Trigger-based follow-ups and reminders.
- Configurable messaging workflows for common lifecycle events.
- AI-assisted communication drafting with human review controls.
- Automation boundaries aligned with account permissions.

### Practical Use Cases
- Automatically send post-appointment follow-up prompts.
- Trigger reminder messages ahead of scheduled services.
- Draft customer responses that staff can review before sending.

## 5. Revenue Intelligence

### Purpose
Improve business decision-making with operational and revenue-oriented visibility.

### Intended Functionality
- Track high-level performance indicators for service operations.
- Surface opportunity gaps tied to communication and booking workflows.
- Provide trend views to support planning and prioritization.

### Practical Use Cases
- Identify where missed follow-ups may be impacting booked revenue.
- Compare conversion patterns across channels and service categories.
- Review appointment pipeline health to inform staffing decisions.

## 6. Command Center

### Purpose
Present a centralized operational snapshot for day-to-day management.

### Intended Functionality
- Aggregate key activity across inbox, bookings, CRM, and automation.
- Highlight urgent tasks and workflow bottlenecks.
- Provide a structured overview for team-level operational decisions.

### Practical Use Cases
- A manager starts the day by reviewing pending conversations and schedule load.
- Teams monitor operational status without manually compiling reports.
- Priority queues help focus effort on the highest-impact actions.
